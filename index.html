Reptiles!

Reptiles are cold-blooded vertebrates that have scaly skin and typically lay eggs. They include snakes, lizards, turtles, and crocodiles, thriving in a variety of habitats ranging from deserts to rainforests. Unlike mammals, reptiles rely on external heat sources to regulate their body temperature. Most reptiles have lungs for breathing and a slow metabolism, which helps them survive in harsh environments. With keen senses and unique adaptations, they play a crucial role in maintaining ecological balance as both predators and prey.
